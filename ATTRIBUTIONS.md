## PokéAPI

The app uses [PokéAPI](https://pokeapi.co/) for Pokémon, move, and held-item
metadata. Pokémon-related intellectual property remains with its respective
rights holders.

## PokéAPI sprites

Pokémon and held-item sprites are loaded from the
[PokéAPI sprite repository](https://github.com/PokeAPI/sprites). The project
does not claim ownership of these assets.

## Pokémon Showdown / Smogon community sprites

The add-Pokémon picker loads form-aware sprites from
[Pokémon Showdown](https://pokemonshowdown.com/) community-hosted sprite paths.
The project does not claim ownership of those sprites or associated Pokémon
intellectual property.

## Riot Games / Teamfight Tactics

The TFT integration retrieves account, match, league, and game data through
Riot Games APIs and loads game imagery from Riot/CommunityDragon data sources
where applicable. Riot Games and its affiliates retain their respective rights.
